The Story of Frivaar

A Play.
A Film.
A Movement.

01

Chapter One

Vishal Gupta Founder

It began with a performance that stopped time. Mr. Vishal Gupta sat in an old age home, watching a play unfold — a story about children who leave their parents behind to chase their dreams. The room was quiet. The air was heavy. Something in him shifted.

"Some stories are too important to stay in one room."

He didn't want that story to end when the curtain fell. He wanted it to travel — to reach families across Punjab, across India, across the world. So he made a film. A short film titled Mukti — liberation, release, peace. And with that, Frivaar Productions was born.

The name itself holds the vision. Frivaar — a bridge between Friday and Shukrvaar, the day every new movie releases in India. The day of new beginnings. The day when stories meet the world.

Short Film · 2020

Mukti
The film that started it all

Written from the heart after witnessing a play at an old age home, Mukti is the story that gave Frivaar Productions its soul. A story about parents, about children who leave, about the silence between them.

This short film was the first proof that Frivaar had something real to say — and that the world was ready to listen.

02

Chapter Two

Aksh Kumar Co-Founder

Not long after Mukti, a second voice joined. Aksh Kumar arrived with a clear mission: Punjabi music deserved a bigger platform. The richness of the language, the emotion in its music, the stories in its folk tradition — all of it needed a vehicle that could carry it to a generation growing up between two worlds.

Together, Vishal and Aksh built something rare — a production house that didn't just make music, but genuinely cared about the artists making it. Frivaar became a launching pad for newcomers. A place where talent didn't need connections or money to be heard.

"We wanted to give artists a stage before the world had decided they deserved one."

Songs like Powered by Jatt, Dimaag Jatt Da, and Jaro Jaro followed — each one representing an artist who got their first real shot because Frivaar believed in them.

03

Chapter Three

Mr. Leo International Partner · Canada

The third chapter brought a third voice — and a whole new horizon. Mr. Leo, joining from Canada as international partner, saw what Frivaar was building and knew it could be something the diaspora would embrace deeply.

Punjabis are everywhere. In London, in Toronto, in Dubai. They carry their music with them — it's identity, it's memory, it's home. Leo's vision was to make Frivaar the bridge between Punjab and every corner of the world where Punjabis had planted roots.

Today, Frivaar Productions operates across two countries, with artists, stories, and sounds that travel far beyond Bassi, Punjab — where it all began.
