The Journey

2020 Year Founded

  • 2020
    Founding

    Frivaar Productions is born

    Vishal Gupta, moved by a play at an old age home in Punjab, creates the short film Mukti and founds Frivaar Productions — named for the day stories meet the world.

  • 2021
    Expansion

    Aksh Kumar joins as co-founder

    With a passion for Punjabi culture and music, Aksh Kumar joins to spearhead the music production arm — opening the house to emerging artists across Punjab.

  • 2022
    First Releases

    First wave of music hits YouTube

    Songs like Dimaag Jatt Da (Rabbi Maan) and Powered by Jatt (Gun Vir) mark Frivaar's debut in music production — showcasing new Punjabi voices on a real stage.

  • 2023
    Growth

    Catalog grows with multiple artists

    A prolific year — White Gold, Jaro Jaro, Mukam, and Trample release across the year, establishing a loyal audience and a growing roster of artists.

  • 2024
    Milestone

    International partner joins from Canada

    Mr. Leo joins as international partner from Canada, signaling Frivaar's ambition to reach the Punjabi diaspora worldwide and scale the production house globally.

  • 2025
    Present

    Frivaar goes international

    With releases like Haryana Te Punjab, Glock, and Jazbati, and a new website reaching fans worldwide — Frivaar Productions steps into its next chapter.
